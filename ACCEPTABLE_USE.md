# Acceptable use

This software lets a business talk with its own customers over iMessage, SMS, email and Slack. It is built so that it **cannot be used to message strangers**, and we ask you not to modify it to do so.

## You may

- Answer people who contacted you first: questions, bookings, reminders they asked for, follow-ups on a conversation they started.
- Hand conversations to your own staff.
- Run it for your own business, or host it for businesses that use it the same way.

## You may not

- Send messages to people who did not contact you first, including lists you bought, scraped or imported. The code refuses to start a conversation with someone who never messaged you; do not remove that check.
- Send marketing to anyone who has not clearly opted in, or keep messaging anyone who replied STOP.
- Impersonate a person, a company or a government, or hide that people are talking to an automated assistant when they ask.
- Send phishing, scams, harassment, threats, or anything illegal where you or the recipient are.
- Use it to collect passwords, payment details, one-time codes or government ids.
- Use many phone lines to get around the limits Apple or carriers place on one number for sending volume.

## Laws that probably apply to you

Depending on where you and your customers are: the TCPA and CTIA guidelines and A2P 10DLC registration in the United States, CASL in Canada, GDPR and ePrivacy in the EU and UK, and similar laws elsewhere. They cover consent, opt-out, identification and record keeping. You are responsible for following them; this is not legal advice.

## Apple

Running iMessage through jailbroken iPhones and a Mac this way is not supported by Apple and may break Apple's terms of service. Apple can change iOS, macOS or iMessage at any time in ways that stop this working, and can restrict numbers or Apple IDs it sees as abusive. Do not build a business that cannot survive that.

## Reporting abuse

If you believe someone is using this software to spam or harm people, report the sending number to your carrier (in the US, forward the text to 7726) and to Apple using Report Junk in Messages. You can also tell us through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
